Speak, and your words appear where you type.

Quoth is dictation for your Mac that runs entirely on your Mac. Hold a key, speak, let go, and the text lands at your cursor, in any app. Nothing is saved, and nothing is sent.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store $1.99 macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon · Also free and open source

Dictation that stays out of your way.

One key, no windows to manage, and the text goes where you were already typing.

Hold to talk

Hold fn, or another modifier key of your choice, speak, and let go. Your words appear at the cursor in any app, usually in well under a second.

Hands-free for long thoughts

Double-tap the key to keep recording without holding it. Text appears each time you pause, and one tap stops.

On your Mac, only

Whisper runs on your Mac's Neural Engine. Your audio never leaves the Mac, and Quoth keeps no recordings and no transcripts.

English, and 98 more

English-only models for speed, or multilingual ones for 99 languages, detected automatically among the languages you speak.

Your words, spelled your way

A dictionary for names and jargon. When Quoth gets a word wrong, Fix Last Dictation teaches it in a few seconds.

Small and quiet

A few megabytes in the menu bar, with the microphone off between dictations. Choose speech models in Settings, and delete the ones you don't use.

How it works

Set up once, then forget it's there.

Allow the microphone and the key

Quoth asks for the microphone, and for permission to notice your dictation key. It only watches modifier keys like fn; it never sees what you type.

Let the model download

The first start downloads a speech model, about 150 MB, once. Larger, more accurate models are a choice away in Settings.

Hold the key and speak

Let go, and the text is at your cursor. For anything longer, double-tap and talk.

Private by design

There is no Quoth account and no Quoth server. Speech is turned into text on your Mac, and the only thing Quoth ever downloads is the speech model you choose.

  • No accounts, no sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or ads
  • Audio is never saved or sent
  • Transcripts are never saved or sent
  • Microphone off between dictations
  • Sees modifier keys only, never your typing

Two ways to get Quoth

The same app and the same speech models, from the store or from the source.

Mac App Store $1.99

Sandboxed by Apple and updated through the App Store. The simplest way to install, and a way to support the work.

  • Pastes at your cursor once you allow it; otherwise each dictation is copied, ready for ⌘V

Free and open source MIT

Download from GitHub, or build it yourself. It can read the text field it types into, which adds a few things:

  • Spacing that fits the text already there
  • Never types into a password field, and notices when you switch fields mid-dictation
  • A quoth command for the terminal

Requirements

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon
  • 150 MB to 1.6 GB of disk space for each speech model you keep
  • A microphone: the Mac's own, a headset, or AirPods
