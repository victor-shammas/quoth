Speak, and your words appear where you type.

Quoth is dictation for your Mac that runs entirely on your Mac. Hold a key, speak, let go, and the text lands at your cursor, in any app. Nothing is saved, and nothing is sent.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store $1.99 macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon

Dictation that stays out of your way.

One key, no windows to manage, and the text goes where you were already typing.

Hold to talk

Hold fn or another modifier key (in the App Store edition, ⌥Space or another key combination), speak, and let go. Your words appear at the cursor in any app, usually in well under a second.

Hands-free for long thoughts

Double-tap the key to keep recording without holding it. Text appears each time you pause; say “new paragraph”, “comma” or “scratch that” as you go; one tap stops.

On your Mac, only

Whisper runs on your Mac's Neural Engine. Your audio never leaves the Mac, and Quoth keeps no recordings and no transcripts.

English, and many more

English-only models for speed, or multilingual ones that know 99 languages, most accurate in widely spoken ones, detected among the languages you speak.

Your words, spelled your way

A dictionary for names and jargon. When Quoth gets a word wrong, Fix Last Dictation teaches it in a few seconds.

The Quote Card

Talk into a small card that floats over your work, fix anything with the keyboard, then press ⌘↩ and it lands where you were. For emails, notes and anything you'd rather read once before it goes in.

How it works

Set up once, then forget it's there.

The microphone, and nothing else you type

Quoth asks for the microphone. The open-source edition also asks for permission to notice your dictation key, and only watches modifier keys like fn. The App Store edition's key combination needs no permission. Neither ever sees what you type.

Let the model download

The first start downloads a speech model, about 150 MB, once. Larger, more accurate models are a choice away in Settings.

Hold the key and speak

Let go, and the text is at your cursor. For anything longer, double-tap and talk.

Private by design

There is no Quoth account and no Quoth server. Speech is turned into text on your Mac, and the only thing Quoth ever downloads is the speech model you choose.

  • No accounts, no sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or ads
  • Audio is never saved or sent
  • Transcripts are never saved or sent
  • Microphone off between dictations
  • Sees modifier keys only, never your typing
